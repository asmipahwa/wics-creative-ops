How to request work

All marketing requests go through the form: WiCS Marketing Request Form

That covers posts, reels, stories, posters, event promos, recaps, sponsor assets and merch. Please don't send requests by DM. DMs get lost, and they don't give anyone an owner or a due date.

To get the best result, have this ready:

  • What you need, in at least 15 words: what it is, who it's for, and what it should make people do.
  • The exact copy: title, date/time, location, links, call to action.
  • References: example posts, photos, sponsor brand guidelines.
  • The event date, if it's for an event.
  • When you need it. Check the lead times below.

What happens after you submit

  1. You get an email right away with your ticket ID, the owner, the due date and the priority.
  2. If everything's there, the ticket goes into Intake and the owner picks it up.
  3. If something's missing, the ticket goes into Needs Info, and the email lists exactly what we need. Reply to that email with the details and your ticket ID.
  4. If your date is sooner than the lead time for that type, the ticket is tagged Rush and the email tells you. We'll do our best, but we may simplify the deliverable or suggest a new date.
  5. If a sponsor or partner is involved, the asset may need their approval before it goes out (see Approvals below).

Questions about status? Reply to your confirmation email with your ticket ID, or check the status lookup on the Dashboard.

Statuses explained

Status Meaning
Intake New and complete. Waiting to be picked up
Needs Info We're waiting on you. See your email for what's missing
In Progress Someone is making it
In Review A draft is ready and being reviewed (by you, the team or a partner)
Approved / Scheduled Signed off and scheduled to post or print
Delivered Posted, printed or sent. Done
Archived Delivered 14+ days ago, or no longer needed. Kept for the record, never deleted

Drive folder map

The organized library is here: WiCS Marketing Library.

WiCS Marketing (Library)/
  00_Brand/            logos, fonts, templates, colour palette, brand guide
  01_Events/<YYYY-YY>/<Event>/{Briefs, WIP, Final, Recap}
  02_Programs/<Program>/{WIP, Final}
  03_Sponsors/<Sponsor>/{Assets, Approvals}
  04_Social/<YYYY-MM>/
  99_Archive/          anything that couldn't be classified, kept not deleted
  • Academic year (YYYY-YY) runs September to August. A file from October 2026 goes in 2026-27.
  • Briefs = requests and notes. WIP = drafts. Final = what actually went out. Recap = photos and posts from after the event.
  • Each request also gets its own working folder (with Briefs / WIP / Final) when it's submitted. The link is on the ticket.

Naming convention

YYYY-MM-DD_Event_AssetType_Channel_vNN.ext

Part Rule Examples
Date The date it's for, or the date it was made 2026-11-08
Event PascalCase, no spaces. General if not for an event TryCATCH, MentorshipProgram, General
AssetType One of: Poster, Post, Story, Reel, Video, Photo, Logo, Banner, Graphic, Slides, Doc, Template, Merch, Other Poster
Channel One of: IG, LI, Discord, Web, Print, Email, Gen IG
Version v01, v02… Bump the version. Never overwrite v02

Examples:

  • 2026-11-08_TryCATCH_Poster_IG_v02.png
  • 2026-10-15_MentorshipProgram_Story_IG_v01.mp4
  • 2026-09-30_General_Logo_Gen_v03.svg
  • 2027-01-20_Sponsor_Banner_LI_v01.png

Why: dates first, so files sort by time. Event and type next, so searching "TryCATCH Poster" finds everything. Versions, so nobody loses the old draft.

How to find assets

Use the Search tab in the report Sheet. Type a word (an event, an asset type, a channel or part of a file name) into the yellow box, and you'll get matching files with links. The Index tab lists every file in the library with its event, type, channel, date, version and link. Use Data → Filter views to narrow it down without changing the view for anyone else.

How sponsor and partner approvals work

If a sponsor or partner is involved (their logo, their event, a post about them), they usually need to sign off before it goes out.

  1. When the form says a sponsor is involved, the ticket is tagged Sponsor and a row goes into the Approvals tab.
  2. We send the draft to the partner: status Submitted, round 1.
  3. If they send feedback: status Changes requested. We note a summary of the feedback and set a follow-up date.
  4. Each new draft we send is the next round (round 2, round 3…).
  5. Once they sign off: status Approved, and the ticket moves to Approved / Scheduled.

Build in time: partners can take a few days per round. That's why sponsor assets have a 10-day lead time.

Onboarding checklist for new marketing members

  • Read this wiki (about 10 minutes).
  • Ask the Director of Marketing to add you to the Asana board and the shared library (view/comment).
  • Bookmark the request form, the Dashboard and the library.
  • Make sure your Asana email is in the Routing tab if you'll own a request type.
  • Look through 00_Brand for logos, fonts, templates and colours.
  • Find one past event in 01_Events to see how Briefs / WIP / Final are used.
  • Save your first file using the naming convention, and ask someone to check it.
  • Submit one test request through the form so you see the email requestors get. Mention it's a test so we can archive it.
  • Join the weekly marketing sync. Action items go in the meeting notes with an owner and a due date.

FAQ

I already DMed someone a request. Do I still need the form? Yes, please. The form is what gives it an owner, a due date and a ticket. It takes about 3 minutes.

My date is sooner than the lead time. Can I still submit? Yes. It'll be tagged Rush and we'll tell you what's realistic.

I got a "Needs Info" email. What now? Reply to it with the missing details and your ticket ID. Once we have them, the ticket moves forward.

How do I check on my request? Reply to your confirmation email, or type your ticket ID into the status lookup on the Dashboard.

I need a file from a past event. Where is it? Search for the event name in the Search tab, or browse 01_Events/<year>/<event>/Final.

Can I delete old files? No. Move them to 99_Archive instead. We never delete from the library.

I don't need my request anymore. Reply to your confirmation email and say so. We'll move it to Archived.
